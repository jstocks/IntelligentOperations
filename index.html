    +25 points per $1M whitespace (max +50). Active account, unexpanded departments, existing relationship.

    ⚡ Why Your Data = SQL, Not MQL
    Your installed base + whitespace + win/loss data bypasses the MQL stage entirely. You already know these accounts, have existing relationships, and have quantified the opportunity. These go straight to Sales Qualified.
    MQL → SQL Conversion (industry avg)
    Cold MQL: 5-15% convert to SQL
    Warm SQL (your data): 35-55% convert to opportunity
    GTM Engine-generated: estimated 40-60% qualification rate

    Existing accounts: SQL directly from this scoring engine
